## The workaround

Land acquisition runs on tabs. A map tool in one, the county records in another, comparable sales in a spreadsheet, the offer template in a document, and the team's questions in a group chat. Every parcel means stitching them together again, and the slowest step is the one that matters most: deciding what to offer.

## What the Land Platform does today

**One map to work from.** The pilot runs on a sample market; connecting live parcel and sales data is the next step. The home screen is a satellite map of the market, with each lead pinned and coloured by priority. Draw an area to get its size, or measure the distance between two points. Open a parcel to see its site details, including flood, contour and structure information, and the team's notes.

**Search the way people actually look.** Find a parcel by owner, address or parcel ID, or say it out loud.

**A price you can check.** The Land Platform starts from the parcel's tax value and blends in comparable sales, weighted by how close and how similar they are. The comparables sit in a table under the estimate, with a confidence score beside it, so the number never arrives without its working.

**Two offers from one price.** From the estimate, the Land Platform writes two offers side by side: a cash offer at a set percentage of value, and a seller-finance offer at a higher price with a small down payment. A buyer adjusts the terms and prints a letter of intent. The letter can't be printed until the buyer ticks a box confirming the terms.

**A team that stays in one place.** Channels, direct messages and private channels live next to the deals, with unread counts, and access is invite-only, with separate roles for acquisitions, sales and admin.

## What's next

The Land Platform is in pilot, on sample data. The first step is live parcel and sales data. After that come the steps that turn a fast screen into a system of record: saving every valuation and offer with its history, a pipeline that hands accepted offers to sales, and an assistant that answers the team's repeat questions from the deal data itself.
